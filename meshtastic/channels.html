Recommended Channels

Channels allow anyone to join and talk, making them great for conversations on specific topics. In order to see messages in a channel, you need to join it first. Your companion will ignore any message sent in any channel you haven't joined. The channels below represent a good place to start, but the channels you join are 100% up to you.

To join a channel below, click "Settings" in your Meshtastic app, then click "Channels", then click the "Plus" (Add) button on the bottom right. Type the channel name in the "Channel Name" field, and the PSK in the "PSK" field. Leave the two MQTT and the Position toggles disabled unless you know what you're doing, then click "Save".

Please respect airtime limits, MQTT bridge settings, and bot guidelines. A big part of keeping our local mesh reliable comes from minimizing unnecessary channel congestion, and keeping MQTT uplinks restricted on secondary channels is one of our best ways of achieving this.
