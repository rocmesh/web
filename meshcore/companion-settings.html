Recommended Companion Settings

Make sure to match these settings on your Companion to ensure you can participate in the mesh!

When setting up your companion, select the "USA/Canada" preset. Depending on your app, you may see "USA/Canada (Recommended)", "Canada, USA", or "USA". The important thing is that your Frequency, Bandwidth, Spreading Factor, and Coding Rate match the below table. If you change the Coding Rate to 8 as suggested below, your Preset will change to "Custom", which is completely fine.

Setting Value
Preset USA/Canada OR Custom
Frequency (MHz) 910.525
Bandwidth 62.5 kHz
Spreading Factor 7
Coding Rate 5 OR 8 (if you have trouble reaching the mesh)
Enable Repeat Mode Unchecked
Default Path Hash Size 2-byte
