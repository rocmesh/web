Recommended Hashtag Channels

"Hashtag Channels" allow anyone to join them, making them great for joining (or ignoring) conversations on specific topics. In order to see messages in Hashtag channels, you need to join them first. Your companion will ignore any message sent in any channel you haven't joined. The channels below represent a good place to start, but the channels you join are 100% up to you.

To join a channel below, click "Add a Channel" in your MeshCore app, then click "Join a Hashtag Channel". For channels without a region scope (unfiltered), that's all you need to do. For channels with a region scope, please click "Show Advanced Settings" and type the region scope in. Once you've added the channel and region scope (if applicable), click "Join Channel", then "Continue to Channel".

Please respect region scopes and bot guidelines. A big part of MeshCore's ROC-solid performance comes from minimizing unnecessary traffic, and these controls are two of our best ways of achieving this.

Channel Name Description Region Scope Bots
Public The channel that everyone lands in when they first set up MeshCore. None No.
#test General radio testing (ping tests, "Can anyone hear me", etc.) us-ny If applicable. For example, ping responders.
#bot The only channel that most bots should send messages in. None Yes.
#roc Rochester-area chat. This is basically our local version of Public. None No.
#emergency Channel for emergency traffic ONLY. No channel is monitored 24/7. If you have an emergency, call 911. None No.
#wardriving This is the channel that the MeshMapper app uses. It's not recommended to join this channel unless you want to get pings when someone is wardriving near you. None Only the MeshMapper app.
