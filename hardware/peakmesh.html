What it is

The Magnet Mover is a finished solar mesh node made by PeakMesh, a small shop on Etsy. It's a low-profile, 3D-printed ASA case sealed with a rubber gasket, with two solar panels on the sloped top and two large 21700 batteries inside. Four neodymium magnets on the base hold it to a car roof, truck or steel building, even at highway speed, and two 36" zip ties are included for mounting it anywhere else.

It's built to order, so you pick the case color, the radio and the antenna. Ours is white, with RAK's high-power kit (the same RAK3401 and RAK13302 boards as our RAK 1W Repeater) and an Alfa 5 dBi antenna. It arrives fully assembled and tested. Attach the antenna, flip the switch and it's on the mesh.

Role in the mesh

Recommended

Mobile node

What it's built for. Stick it on your car roof and it relays traffic and keeps you connected on the road, with the solar panels keeping it charged.

Recommended

Repeater

Magnets or zip ties let you put it almost anywhere high, and up to a month of battery gets it through bad weather. For tall poles and trees, PeakMesh also sells vertical versions (the Magnet Climber and Summit).

Not recommended

Pocket companion

It works as a companion, but it's too big to carry around. For a node that lives in your pocket, get a Heltec T114 or T1000-E.

Where to buy and what it costs

PeakMesh builds each one to order, and the price depends on the options you choose:

  • Radio: no radio (bring your own), the standard RAK4631 (lowest power use), or the RAK high-power kit (more range, more power).
  • Antenna: none (bring your own N-type antenna), the Alfa 2 dBi stubby, or the Alfa 5 dBi.
  • Color: six choices. 3D-printed colors can vary slightly from the photos.

Shipping within the US is free. PeakMesh updates its listings as inventory changes, so if the link stops working, check the shop for the current version.

Setup

  1. Attach the antenna before you switch it on. Running the radio without an antenna can damage it.
  2. Press the power switch. It glows faint blue while the radio is on.
  3. After about 20 seconds, connect from the Meshtastic app over Bluetooth. The default PIN is 123456. Change it once you're connected.
  4. Follow our recommended settings for how you'll use it.

It ships with Meshtastic. If you'd rather run MeshCore, ask PeakMesh to flash it when you order. MeshCore repeaters don't use Bluetooth, so you change their settings over the mesh instead. If you reflash it yourself, choose the RAK3401 1-watt firmware so the booster works properly. The high-power kit uses more battery, so in darker spots you may need to adjust its settings to keep it charged.
