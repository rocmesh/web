Heltec ESP32V4

The Heltec V3's successor, with a much stronger radio, solar input and a GPS connector, for about the same price.

MeshCore Meshtastic Home base High power ~$18-28

What it is

The Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 is the newest version of the popular Heltec V3. It keeps the same idea: an ESP32-S3 chip with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, a Semtech SX1262 LoRa radio and a small 0.96" OLED screen. The V4 adds a power amplifier that roughly quintuples transmit power (28 dBm, up from 21 dBm on the V3), a connector for a solar panel and a plug-in port for a GPS module.

It's sold as a bare board with a basic LoRa antenna. You add a case and, if you want to carry it or run it on solar, a battery. The size and pin layout are close to the V3, but many V3 cases and add-ons don't fit or work, so check before you reuse them.

Role in the mesh

Recommended

Home base companion

Plug it into USB power near a window and leave it on. The stronger radio reaches further than the V3, the screen shows new messages at a glance, and Wi-Fi lets you connect from your computer.

Works

Solar repeater

Built-in solar charging and high transmit power make it a capable repeater, but the ESP32 needs a bigger panel and battery than nRF52 boards. For a simple outdoor site, see the Basic Repeater.

Works

Pocket companion

Add a battery and a case and it makes a capable handheld, and there are plenty of case designs to choose from. Expect to charge it daily, though: the ESP32 and high-power radio drain batteries much faster than nRF52 boards. For multi-day battery life, see the Heltec T114 or T1000-E.

Where to buy and what it costs

Always choose the 902-928 MHz band for use in the US. Heltec sells two chip options, the standard V4 and the V4-R8 with more memory. Either works, but note which one you bought, because each needs its own firmware. Pick the US warehouse for faster shipping.

Assembly notes

  1. Flash the firmware before putting it in the case (see Setup below).
  2. Connect the antenna to the IPEX connector. Press straight down until it clicks. Never power the radio without an antenna attached.
  3. Step three.

Setup

Flash the firmware from a web browser (Chrome or Edge) with the Heltec V4 plugged in over USB-C, then follow our recommended settings. In the flasher, pick Heltec V4 or Heltec V4-R8 to match the board you bought. Flashing the wrong one won't work. If the flasher can't find the board, hold the PRG button, press RST once, then let go of PRG and try again.

  • MeshCore: MeshCore web flasher, then see MeshCore companion settings.
  • Meshtastic: Meshtastic web flasher, then see Meshtastic companion settings.
