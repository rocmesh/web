Seeed SenseCAP Card Tracker T1000-E

A slim, waterproof, credit-card-sized tracker with built-in GPS and a buzzer for alerts. No assembly needed.

MeshCore Meshtastic Companion Low power ~$40-50

What it is

The SenseCAP Card Tracker T1000-E is a ready-made, credit-card-sized LoRa tracker from Seeed Studio, and one of the most popular plug-and-play mesh devices. Inside is Nordic's low-power nRF52840 chip, a Semtech LR1110 radio, a MediaTek GPS and a 700 mAh battery, all sealed in a weatherproof case. It connects to your phone over Bluetooth, and you use the MeshCore or Meshtastic app to send and read messages.

There's nothing to build. It arrives with the battery and antennas inside, and charges with the included magnetic cable. There's no screen, so you set it up from the app. A single button, an LED and a buzzer give you status and alerts.

Role in the mesh

Recommended

Companion

Pair it with your phone over Bluetooth and keep it in a wallet, pocket or bag. It's the easiest way onto the mesh: no soldering, no case and no antenna to choose.

Recommended

Location tracker

Built-in GPS and a slim, waterproof body make it a good fit for hiking, events, or sharing your position with family on the mesh. Double-press the button to send your position.

Not recommended

Repeater

The small internal antenna can't be upgraded, and the 700 mAh battery is sized for carrying, not for running all the time. For a fixed site, use the Basic Repeater.

Setup

Flash the firmware from a web browser (Chrome or Edge) with the T1000-E connected by its magnetic USB cable, then follow our recommended settings.

  • MeshCore: MeshCore web flasher, then see MeshCore companion settings.
  • Meshtastic: Meshtastic web flasher, then see Meshtastic companion settings.
