M5Stack Cardputer ADV

A pocket computer with a real keyboard and screen. Running Plai, it's a standalone Meshtastic messenger that doesn't need a phone.

MeshCore Meshtastic Standalone Keyboard GPS ~$48-65

What it is

The Cardputer ADV is a credit-card-sized ESP32-S3 computer from M5Stack, with a 56-key keyboard, a small color screen and a built-in battery. M5Stack's Cardputer Mesh Kit adds a snap-on Cap LoRa-1262, which gives it a Semtech SX1262 LoRa radio and a GPS, and ships with Meshtastic installed.

Ours runs Plai, a community-made, standalone Meshtastic messenger. You read and type messages right on the device, with no phone or app needed, and your messages and contacts are saved to the microSD card. Thanks to Launcher (formerly M5Launcher), you can keep Meshtastic and MeshCore on the same SD card and switch between them.

Role in the mesh

Recommended

Standalone messenger

What it does best. With a keyboard and screen built in, you can read and send messages without a phone. That's great when your phone is dead, or if you'd rather keep it in your pocket.

Works

Phone companion

With the stock Meshtastic or MeshCore firmware, it pairs with your phone like any other node. You'd be carrying a keyboard you don't need, though. For a smaller companion, see the T1000-E.

Not recommended

Repeater

It can relay, but the small battery and power-hungry ESP32 won't last, and the keyboard and screen go to waste. For a fixed site, use the RAK 1W Repeater.

Where to buy and what it costs

Buy the Cardputer Mesh Kit, which includes the Cardputer ADV and the Cap LoRa-1262. The Cardputer on its own has no LoRa radio. You'll also need a microSD card for Plai and Launcher.

Setup

The Mesh Kit ships with Meshtastic installed and works out of the box. To run Plai, or to switch between Meshtastic and MeshCore, set up Launcher first:

  1. Flash Launcher to the Cardputer with the Launcher web flasher (Chrome or Edge, plugged in over USB-C).
  2. Format a microSD card as FAT32 (32 GB or smaller is safest) and copy on the firmware files you want: Plai, Meshtastic and MeshCore.
  3. Put the card in the Cardputer and turn it on. Launcher lets you pick a firmware from the SD card and install it. After that, the Cardputer starts straight into that firmware unless you press a key during startup to get back to Launcher.
  4. Follow our recommended settings for whichever firmware you're running.
  • MeshCore: MeshCore web flasher, then see MeshCore companion settings.
  • Meshtastic: Meshtastic web flasher, then see Meshtastic companion settings.
