Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 Expansion Kit

A ready-made handheld built around the Heltec V4, with a big screen, a metal case, a full-size antenna and room for an 18650 battery. No assembly needed.

MeshCore Meshtastic Companion High power ~$50-59

What it is

The Expansion Kit is Heltec's finished handheld version of the WiFi LoRa 32 V4. The V4 board comes pre-installed on an expansion board inside an aluminum and polycarbonate case, with a 320 x 240 color screen on the front. It keeps the V4's strengths: an ESP32-S3 chip with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and a Semtech SX1262 radio with up to 28 dBm of transmit power.

There's nothing to build. It comes with a LoRa antenna on an SMA connector, and you can order it with a 2800 mAh 18650 battery, a GPS module and a touch screen. The case isn't waterproof, so keep it out of the rain.

Role in the mesh

Recommended

Handheld companion

What it's built for. The big screen lets you read messages without your phone, the 18650 battery gives it real staying power, and the full-size antenna and high-power radio reach further than pocket trackers.

Recommended

Home base companion

Leave it plugged into USB on a desk or shelf. The screen shows new messages at a glance, and Wi-Fi lets you connect from your computer.

Not recommended

Repeater

It works as a repeater, but the case isn't waterproof and you'd be paying for a screen nobody will look at. For a fixed site, the Basic Repeater or RAK 1W Repeater is a better fit.

Where to buy and what it costs

Choose the 863-928 MHz band, which covers the US 915 MHz band, and the 28 dBm version for the best range. The GPS module, touch screen and battery each add to the price. Ordering the battery also raises shipping costs, so it's often cheaper to buy an 18650 locally.

Setup

Flash the firmware from a web browser (Chrome or Edge) with the kit plugged in over USB-C, then follow our recommended settings. The kit uses the standard V4 board, not the V4-R8, so pick the matching V4 entry in the flasher. If the flasher can't find it, hold the USER button, press RST once, then let go of USER and try again.

  • MeshCore: MeshCore web flasher. Choose Heltec v4 + Expansion Kit (Touch). The screen works, but touch input isn't supported yet. Then see MeshCore companion settings.
  • Meshtastic: Meshtastic web flasher. Use version 2.7.20 or newer, and turn on the Meshtastic UI option if you have the touch screen. Then see Meshtastic companion settings.
