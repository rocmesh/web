RAK WisMesh Pocket V2

A rugged, ready-made handheld with a screen, GPS, a big battery and a swappable antenna. One of the easiest all-in-one ways onto the mesh.

MeshCore Meshtastic Companion Low power GPS ~$90

What it is

The WisMesh Pocket V2 is RAKwireless's finished handheld node. Inside is RAK's WisBlock RAK4631 core, which pairs Nordic's low-power nRF52840 chip with a Semtech SX1262 LoRa radio. Around it are a 1.3" screen, GPS, an accelerometer and a 3200 mAh battery, all in a screwed-together case with a power switch and a user button.

There's nothing to build. It ships with Meshtastic installed and a 2 dBi antenna on an SMA connector, so you can screw on a bigger antenna later. V2 updates the original Pocket with a new board, better power and battery circuits, and the SMA connector.

Works

Repeater

Low power use, a big battery and an SMA antenna make it a solid indoor or temporary repeater. It isn't weatherproof, though, so for an outdoor site use the RAK 1W Repeater.

Where to buy and what it costs

Setup

  1. Charge it fully before the first use. Use a 5 V / 2 A wall charger rather than a computer's USB port, and it charges faster with the power switch off.
  2. Make sure the antenna is screwed on tightly, then turn it on with the power switch.
  3. Connect from the Meshtastic app over Bluetooth, then follow our recommended settings.

To switch to MeshCore or update Meshtastic, plug it in over USB-C and use a web flasher (Chrome or Edge). Choose the standard RAK4631 firmware. Don't use the RAK4631 E-Ink version, which is for a different screen.

  • MeshCore: MeshCore web flasher, then see MeshCore companion settings.
  • Meshtastic: Meshtastic web flasher, then see Meshtastic companion settings.
