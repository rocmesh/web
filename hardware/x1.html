Seeed SenseCAP MeshTracker X1

A rugged, waterproof, credit-card-sized tracker with dual-band GPS and up to five days of battery life. No assembly needed.

MeshCore Meshtastic Companion Low power ~$50

What it is

The SenseCAP MeshTracker X1 is a ready-made, credit-card-sized LoRa tracker from Seeed Studio. Inside is Nordic's low-power nRF52840 chip, Semtech's newer LR2021 radio, dual-band GPS and a 1100 mAh battery, all sealed in a waterproof case. It connects to your phone over Bluetooth, and you use the MeshCore or Meshtastic app to send and read messages.

There's nothing to build. It arrives with the battery and antennas inside, and charges over USB-C. There's no screen, so you set it up from the app. A single button, an LED, a buzzer and a vibration motor give you status and alerts.

Role in the mesh

Recommended

Companion

Pair it with your phone over Bluetooth and keep it in a wallet, pocket or bag. It's the easiest way onto the mesh: no soldering, no case and no antenna to choose.

Recommended

Location tracker

Dual-band GPS gives accurate fixes even between buildings. It's a good fit for hiking, events, or sharing your position with family on the mesh. Double-press the button to send your position.

Not recommended

Repeater

The small internal antenna can't be upgraded, and the battery is sized for carrying, not for running all the time. For a fixed site, use the Basic Repeater.
