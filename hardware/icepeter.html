Icepeter

A Heltec V3 and a 3000 mAh battery packed into an empty Talenti gelato jar. Toss a rope over a branch, hoist it up a tree, and you have an instant temporary repeater.

MeshCore Meshtastic Repeater ~$20 + V3

What it is

The Icepeter is our most low-tech build. A Heltec V3 and a 3000 mAh LiPo battery go into an empty Talenti jar, braced with paper towels so nothing rattles around. An eyelet screwed into the lid gives you something to tie a rope to, and the antenna hangs straight down below the jar.

It's built to be thrown into a tree and hoisted up as high as you can get it, then taken down when you're done. The jar is light, tough and closes tightly, and you get to eat the gelato first.

ProcessorESP32-S3 on the Heltec V3 (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE 5.0)
RadioSemtech SX1262 LoRa, 902-928 MHz (US)
Antenna915 MHz SMA antenna on a u.FL pigtail, hanging below the jar
BatteryMakerFocus 3000 mAh LiPo, charged over the V3's USB-C port
CaseAn empty Talenti gelato jar, braced inside with paper towels
MountingEyelet in the lid, for a rope or cord
WeatherproofingThe jar keeps out light rain, but it isn't sealed
FirmwareMeshCore, Meshtastic

Role in the mesh

Recommended

Temporary tree repeater

What it's built for. At campsites, events or emergencies, hoist it up a tree to get over the terrain and fill a coverage gap. It runs until the battery is empty, so charge it fully before you go.

Not recommended

Permanent repeater

With no solar and a power-hungry ESP32, it'll need to come down to recharge. For one that stays up, see the Heltec V3 Solar Repeater or RAK 1W Repeater.

Not recommended

Companion

It'll pair with a phone, but a gelato jar isn't something you want to carry around. For a handheld on the same board, see the Heltec V3 Handheld.

Assembly notes

  1. Flash the firmware first (see Setup below).
  2. Screw the eyelet through the center of the lid.
  3. Connect the antenna and run it out the bottom of the jar so it hangs straight down. Never power the radio without an antenna attached.
  4. Check the battery's polarity, plug it into the V3, and pack both into the jar with paper towels so nothing moves when it swings.
  5. Screw the lid on, tie on your rope and hoist it up.

Setup

Flash the firmware from a web browser (Chrome or Edge) with the Heltec V3 plugged in over USB-C, then follow our recommended settings. To make the battery last, turn off Wi-Fi and the screen once it's set up.
