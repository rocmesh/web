Heltec V3 Handheld

A Heltec V3 and a LiPo battery in a snap-together case. A pocket-sized node with a screen, built in minutes with no soldering.

MeshCore Meshtastic Companion Wi-Fi ~$77

What it is

This is the quickest way to turn a Heltec V3 into a handheld. The board and a LiPo battery go into a ready-made blue case, with a flexible whip antenna on the side. The V3's screen shows through a window in the front, so you can read messages at a glance or connect from your phone.

There's no soldering and no 3D printing. The V3 plugs into the battery, the case snaps shut, and it charges over USB-C.

Role in the mesh

Works

Pocket companion

It's small enough to carry, and the screen means you don't always need your phone. Expect to charge it daily, though: the ESP32 drains batteries much faster than nRF52 boards. For multi-day battery life, see the Heltec T114.

Recommended

Home base companion

Leave it plugged into USB near a window. The screen shows new messages, and Wi-Fi lets you connect from your computer as well as your phone.

Not recommended

Repeater

It'll relay, but the case isn't weatherproof and the small battery won't last. For a solar repeater built on the same board, see the Heltec V3 Solar Repeater.

Assembly notes

  1. Flash the firmware first (see Setup below).
  2. Connect the antenna to the V3. Never power the radio without an antenna attached.
  3. Check the battery's polarity against the V3's battery connector, then plug it in.
  4. Fit the V3 and battery into the case and snap it shut.

Setup

Connect the antenna before you power it on. Powering a LoRa radio without an antenna attached, even just over USB to flash it, can permanently damage it.

Flash the firmware from a web browser (Chrome or Edge) with the Heltec V3 plugged in over USB-C, then follow our recommended settings. See the Heltec V3 page for more on the board itself.

  • MeshCore: MeshCore web flasher, then see MeshCore companion settings.
  • Meshtastic: Meshtastic web flasher, then see Meshtastic companion settings.
